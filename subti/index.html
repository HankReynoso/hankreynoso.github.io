CCSubti

Captions in two languages

Record or import short clips, get Spanish or English captions with a translated second line, and export a vertical video ready for TikTok, Reels and Shorts. Everything happens on your iPhone.

Coming soon to the App Store

How it works

  • Record clips in the app or import them from Photos, then trim and reorder them.
  • The app writes captions word by word as you speak, and adds a translated line in the other language.
  • Fix any word or timing, pick a caption style, and export a 9:16 video with the captions built in.
