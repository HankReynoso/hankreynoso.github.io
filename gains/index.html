Gains

Earn your fun money

Set a weekly exercise goal and what meeting it is worth. Every workout earns you permission to spend your own fun money; go past your goal for a bonus.

Download on the App Store

How it works

  • Log workouts by hand, or let Apple Health log them for you with Gains Plus.
  • Minutes × intensity become Effort Points. Hit your weekly Quota to earn your full Base Wage.
  • Spend from your Balance, keep a Streak going, earn Badges, and cheer on Friends.

Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email hankrey@icloud.com.

To manage or cancel Gains Plus, open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions.

Privacy

Your information stays on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. No ads, no tracking. Read the Privacy Policy.
